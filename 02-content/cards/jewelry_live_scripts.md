**Standard Response:**
"This beauty is only $[XX]! And right now we have a special deal — use code [CODE] for extra savings!"

**Value-Added Response:**
"It's $[XX] but honestly? The quality is insane! This would retail for $50+ anywhere else!"

**Urgency Response:**
"[Price]! And we're only doing this price for the next 5 minutes — after that, back to $[XX]!"

### Responding to "Is This Real Gold/Silver?" (回应"是真的金/银吗"问题)

**Gold Response:**
"This is 18K gold-plated over high-quality stainless steel — it won't tarnish, won't fade, and looks AMAZING! Some people literally think it's real gold!"

**Silver Response:**
"This is real sterling silver! Stamped and everything — you'll get a certificate of authenticity with this piece!"

**Quality Clarification:**
"I totally get it — let me explain! This is gold-plated, which means we put a thick layer of real gold over stainless steel. It's different from gold-filled, but the quality is amazing and it lasts for years!"

---

### Responding to "What's Your Best Price?" (回应"最低价多少"问题)
